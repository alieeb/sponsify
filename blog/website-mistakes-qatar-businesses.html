7 Website Mistakes That Cost Qatar Businesses Customers (and How to Fix Them)

SPONSIFY team · 06 November 2026 · 2 min read

Your website is often the first place a customer decides whether to trust you. These are the mistakes we see most often on business websites in Qatar, and how to fix each one.

1. It is slow on mobile

Most visitors arrive on their phone, often from an Instagram or Google ad. Heavy images and videos make the page crawl, and people leave before it loads. Compress images, use modern formats like WebP, and load videos only when needed.

2. No Arabic version, or a machine-translated one

A large share of customers in Qatar prefer Arabic. A rough automatic translation can hurt trust more than having none. Write proper Arabic pages and mirror the layout for right-to-left reading.

3. Contacting you takes too many steps

If a customer has to hunt for your number, you lose them. Add a visible WhatsApp button, a tap-to-call number and a short form on every page.

4. The headline says nothing

"Welcome to our website" tells visitors nothing. Your first line should say what you do, for whom, and why you are the better choice, in under ten words.

5. No proof

Visitors look for signs that others trust you: client logos, real work, reviews and clear before and after examples. Put them near the top, not hidden on a separate page.

6. Google can't understand it

Missing page titles, missing descriptions and images without alt text make it harder to rank. Add a clear title and description to every page, submit a sitemap in Google Search Console, and describe your services in plain words.

7. It is never updated

Old offers, broken links and last year's photos tell customers you might not be active. Review your site every month and publish something new, such as a project, an article or an offer.

A quick self-check

  • Open your site on your phone with mobile data. Does it load fast?
  • Can you reach WhatsApp in one tap from any page?
  • Does the first line explain what you do?
  • Is there an Arabic version that reads naturally?
