How to Choose the Best Digital Marketing Agency in Qatar: 10 Questions to Ask

SPONSIFY team · 06 November 2026 · 2 min read

Search "best marketing agency in Qatar" and you will get a long list of names, each promising growth. The hard part is telling them apart before you sign a contract. These ten questions help you do that in one meeting.

1. What will you measure, and how often will I see it?

A good agency talks about calls, bookings, orders and cost per customer. Likes and impressions are useful signals, but they don't pay salaries. Ask to see a sample monthly report before you start.

2. Can I see work you did for a business like mine?

Look for real work in your sector or a close one. A restaurant, a clinic and a B2B software company need very different marketing. Ask what the brief was and what changed after the campaign.

3. Do you create content in both Arabic and English?

Many Qatar audiences switch between Arabic and English every day. Translated captions rarely land as well as copy written natively in each language. Ask who writes the Arabic, and ask to read some.

4. Who will actually work on my account?

Some agencies sell with senior people and deliver with juniors. Ask who designs, who writes, who manages ads, and who you message when something breaks.

5. Who owns the ad accounts, the website and the content?

Your Meta Business account, Google Ads account, domain and website should be in your name, with the agency added as a partner. If you leave, you keep everything.

6. How do you decide the ad budget?

The right answer starts from your goal and your margins, not from a fixed package. Ask how they would split budget between testing and scaling, and when they would stop a campaign that isn't working.

7. What happens in the first 30 days?

You want a clear plan: an audit, tracking set up, first creatives, first campaigns, and a review date. Vague answers here usually mean vague results later.

8. How do you handle tracking?

Without proper tracking, nobody can tell which ad brought which customer. Ask about the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, Google Analytics, call tracking and WhatsApp click tracking.

9. What is in the contract?

Check the scope, the number of deliverables, the notice period and what happens to unused budget. Clear scope and clear pricing protect both sides.

10. What would you fix first in my business today?

This is the best test. A strong agency will look at your website, ads and social accounts and give you two or three specific fixes on the spot. If all you hear is a sales pitch, keep looking.

The short version

  • Choose results you can measure over follow counts.
  • Insist on owning your accounts and assets.
  • Expect native Arabic and English content.
  • Judge the agency by what they would fix first.
