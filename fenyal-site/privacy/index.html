Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal information we receive through this website, why, and the choices you have.

01Who we are

This website belongs to Fenyal, a café on Crystal Walk, Gewan Island, Doha, Qatar. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Fenyal. We are responsible for the personal information you share with us.

02What we collect

We only receive personal information you choose to send us:

  • Reservation requests: your name, phone number, date, time, number of guests, occasion and any notes you add.
  • Messages: your name, phone number and the content of your message.
  • Calls: what you tell us when you phone the café.

The forms on this website do not store anything on our servers. When you press send, they open WhatsApp with your message filled in, and nothing reaches us until you send it there.

This website does not use accounts, analytics or advertising cookies.

03How we use it

  • To confirm, change or cancel your reservation.
  • To answer your questions and plan events or large groups.
  • To prepare for allergies or special requests you tell us about.
  • To improve our service based on your feedback.

04Services we rely on

Some parts of this website are provided by other companies, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): used to send reservation requests and messages.
  • Google Maps: the map on our Contact page is loaded from Google.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces on this website are loaded from Google.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

05How long we keep it

We keep reservation and message details only as long as we need them to look after your visit or answer your request, and then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

06Your rights

We handle personal information in line with Qatar’s Law No. 13 of 2016 on Personal Data Privacy Protection. You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or stop using it.

To make a request, contact us by phone on +974 7081 3711 or through our Contact page.

07Children

This website is not aimed at children. Reservations should be made by an adult.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Questions about this page? Contact us or call +974 7081 3711.
