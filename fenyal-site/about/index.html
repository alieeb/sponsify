Our story

The dishes Qatari homes grew up on.

A fenyal is the small cup Arabic coffee is poured into. It gave us our name, and our way of cooking.

That cup sets the tone for everything we serve: simple, generous food, made the way it is made at home, in a bright café on Crystal Walk, Gewan Island.

In 2026 the MICHELIN Guide Doha recognised Fenyal with a Bib Gourmand, the distinction for good food at a fair price. For us it is a reminder to keep the classics honest: balaleet sweet and saffron-yellow, foul slow and rich, karak strong.

A day at Fenyal

From sunrise to late evening.

07:00

Morning starts sweet.

Saffron balaleet beneath a thin omelette, slow-cooked foul and bread fresh from the oven, with Arabic coffee from the dallah.

15:00

Afternoon, warm from the oven.

Cheese fatayer and pull-apart honeycomb hives with za'atar, spinach or lamb, with a strong karak on the side.

21:00

Evening, the full table.

Chicken nashif, lamb kofta and ribs over hummus, then saffron elbah to finish.
